The letters spell LIFE IS PASSING ME BY. Don't fast travel or switch characters mid-way, or restart at stop 1. The last switch sends a text 'U-235 REACTOR2 19-06': enter the Reactor 2 metal door at LPL Turtle Head Power Station (east of Hamlet) between 19:00 and 06:00. Rewards: Tommy Vercetti's blue Hawaiian shirt, a Western Malibu Zombie Bobber chopper and a Vice Vinyl livery.📍 Map pin
