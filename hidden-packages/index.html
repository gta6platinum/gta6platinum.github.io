Easter Egg: Vice City Portal (Tommy Vercetti's shirt)Start at the Badger R&D radio tower in Key Lento (north of Nifty Threads Island Wear): in the office with the TV, pull the power lever, then press the button next to the TV. Then visit the 12 LPL substations in this order (letter on the wall → input; Circle = D, Square = A, Triangle = W, L1 = dot): 1 Downtown Vice City L→D · 2 Balsero iF→D · 3 Key Lento Ei→dot · 4 north Vice Beach, behind the hospital S→A · 5 North Vice City P→dot lever on the outside wall · 6 Port Gellhorn A→A · 7 Hamlet Ss→A · 8 Gloriana Key iN→A · 9 West Hamlet G→dot lever · 10 East Port Gellhorn Me→W · 11 Hamlet B→D · 12 near Breakwater Quarry, southwest of the airport Y→W. The letters spell LIFE IS PASSING ME BY. Don't fast travel or switch characters mid-way, or restart at stop 1. The last switch sends a text 'U-235 REACTOR2 19-06': enter the Reactor 2 metal door at LPL Turtle Head Power Station (east of Hamlet) between 19:00 and 06:00. Rewards: Tommy Vercetti's blue Hawaiian shirt, a Western Malibu Zombie Bobber chopper and a Vice Vinyl livery.📍 Map pin
