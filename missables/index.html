GTA 6 Missables — Everything You Can Miss, Chapter by Chapter

GTA 6 has no mission replay, so some things are gone for good once you move on. Here is everything to clear before each chapter ends.

✅ Track these in the free checklist →

The checklist saves your progress on your phone, works in English, Arabic and Spanish, and has every other collectible too.

Chapter 1

  1. Make a manual saveKeep one save in Chapter 1 that you never overwrite.
  2. Balcony meditation with Lucia ⚠️ MISSABLEAfter Down to Earth, talk to Lucia inside Jason's house until she goes to meditate on the balcony, then accept. Gone after Chapter 1.📍 Map pin
  3. Repair Jason's house ⚠️ MISSABLEDo the optional repairs at Jason's house (tools are already there) before picking up Lucia in Coming Home.
  4. Lab Fees codesMoonstone Boutique back room 0420 · Brian's Lockup 7700.📍 Map pin
  5. Kenton's underwater treasureKenton unlocks after Island Dreams and pauses after Chapter 1 — dive his shipwrecks now if you can.📍 Map pin
  6. Grab the free legendary weaponsHunter's Laird 350 at the Grassrivers junction and the Keys pickups are available from the start — see the Weapons tab.📍 Map pin
  7. Start collecting cardsCards appear after Gone Fishing. Key Lento has several — search “Key Lento” in the Cards tab.
  8. Magazines in Jason's house ⚠️ MISSABLE5 magazines/books at Jason's house disappear when Chapter 1 ends — grab them (see the video).
  9. Bank your cash before Tourist Trap 💰Tourist Trap is the point of no return: it locks you into Tourist Trap → Discharge Time → Chapter 2 with no free roam, and Jason's house and everything in it are gone. Before starting it, move your cash into Family Savings (Buck Yourself in BuckMe; first goal $5,000) — chapter 1 wipes the cash you carry. Also grab the house magazines and do the balcony meditation (the same magazine titles also appear elsewhere).
  10. Hey Arnold!: kill or spare ArnoldKilling him on the dock pushes Jason toward a Hardened profile; letting him go keeps Jason Restrained and Arnold may come back later. Your profile is reported to feed into the ending.
  11. Get Shirley's Rum ⚠️ MISSABLEOptional mission that disappears once Tourist Trap starts (Steal the Scooter opens after it). Not needed for trophies or 100%, but it gives a Focus increase.
  12. Ultimate Edition: motorcycle & kayakIf you own the Ultimate Edition, the motorcycle and kayak kept at Jason's house are reported usable only in Chapter 1 — enjoy them now.
  13. What Tourist Trap takes awayTourist Trap locks you into the end of Chapter 1: you lose the house, unique weapons, wallet cash, personal vehicles, floor stash, some fitness and the Lucia relationship level. Finish everything first.

Chapter 2

  1. Vicki card — Starlet Motel officeDelights Dancers set: on the floor by the toilet in the motel office bathroom. The room locks after Chapter 2, but GTABase lists a second spot for this card (its map pin opens it) — grab it here anyway.
  2. Victor Maravilla card — motel kitchenLos Santos Corkers set: on the fridge in the Starlet Motel kitchen. The kitchen locks after Chapter 2, but there's a second copy on the bench next to the Ambrosia baseball field.
  3. Two magazines in the motelTwo magazine pickups inside the Starlet Motel are gone after Chapter 2 — the same magazines can be found elsewhere, but grab them now.
  4. Checking Out needs $15,000Checking Out is the point of no return: it needs every other Chapter 2 main mission done and at least $15,000. The motel stops being your home after it.
  5. Not missable: Delights card & DunkThe card inside Delights can be picked up later because the club reopens, and the fence Dunk leaves for a while but comes back.

Chapter 3

  1. Join a Street Race → Mentor Yago → Race Against Yago ⚠️ TIMEDJoin a Street Race unlocks right after Dealer's Choice: race at the Salton lot (1), then the La Perle station meetup shows up — race there (2). After that Yago's next mission, Mentor Yago (Bumper to Bumper), appears. Yago's chain: Join a Street Race → Mentor Yago (Bumper to Bumper) → Race Against Yago (a few in-game days later Yago texts Jason; timed and missable — go straight away and pick Freeway Run at Salton) → Brother to Brother. Needed for Strangers & Freaks. The other Hit 'n' Run VC races (Ocean Beach, Gloriana Key) aren't timed — do them any time for the race trophies.📍 Map pin
  2. Get the Key ClonerClonin' Keys, Makin' GS (steal the Zorrusso for Randy) gives you the Key Cloner and Randy's Wish List.📍 Map pin
  3. Randy's Wish List carsHigh-end cars, $30–35k each, one-time list of 22 (the last one, the Truffade Thrax, comes by phone once the others are in) — all listed in the Special cars tab. Every car has an Advanced Tracker: deliver to Randy in Balsero with zero damage.📍 Map pin
  4. Register tracked cars at Pay 'n' SprayFrom early Chapter 3, Pay 'n' Spray registers cars with a Standard Tracker.
  5. Bank the end-of-chapter payoutA story payout of about $120,000 is reported at the end of Chapter 3 — bank it.
  6. Trophy: Bad Luck, I GuessUnlocks after Credit Check (Chapter 3), which gives you the Dye Pack Jammer. Go alone as Lucia to the Payday Loans store in Little Cuba (the prologue store), rob it, and lose the police ON FOOT: no car, bike or boat, or it won't count. Security is high: wear a mask, and take out the guard ahead and the one on the right, then use the snap gun on the side door.📍 Map pin
  7. Vanessa's free garage + pink Growler ⚠️ MISSABLEIn the Bayside apartment (after Welcome to Vice City), unlock the bedroom laptop with the password Muffins / Queen / 15, read the WhatUp message from Unknown, then enter 92665 on the keypad of the pink-bow garage next to Auntie's Place, La Perle (Del Rio Blvd). You get a free garage and Vanessa's pink Pfister Growler. Gone once Chapter 3 ends.📍 Map pin
  8. Say yes to Halifax (Dubious Provenance) ⚠️ MISSABLEHand Halifax the statue. If you refuse, an optional call stays open only until Chapter 3 ends — without it, Parts 2 and 3, their cash and the '32 Hustler are gone.
  9. Monty's 1992 Pistol (The Missing Link) ⚠️ MISSABLEOn the floor of the photo room during The Missing Link. Pick it up before you leave — missions can't be replayed.
  10. Boobie's bricks (Bricked Up) ⚠️ MISSABLEGrab the red bag near the end of the table in the first room: $3,500 and the Professional bonus. Leave that room without it and both are gone.
  11. Bayside apartment magazines (2) ⚠️ MISSABLEPassion B.C. next to the bathtub and Two Pricks in the Bush on the table in front of the TV. The apartment is gone after Chapter 3.
  12. Dealer's Choice: pick “Pure”The buyers notice if you bring “Cut”. Choose “Pure” to finish the deal without a fight.
  13. Meet the Heders: order surf and turfLucia tells you her order on the way in. Remember it at dinner (surf and turf) to boost your relationship.
  14. Kyle's last mission: decide his fateBacking the police pays $5,000. Letting Kyle win means giving up that payout (players report Kyle then clears some biker gang spots for you).
  15. Safe Word: turn the director downWhen the director asks Jason to take part, decline. Agreeing hurts his relationship with Lucia.
  16. Hidden Charges: finish all three towersPlant the charges together to save time and build your bond. Doing all three gives you nearly 11 minutes during the heist.

Chapter 4

  1. Manual save at the startKeep one untouched save per chapter.
  2. Free garage: Thompson CourtMission Company Cars gives you Thompson Court Garage (Ocean Beach).📍 Map pin
  3. Keep buying garagesLittle Boxes needs all 8 purchasable garages — see the Garage tab.
  4. Bad Driver! — find Felipe ⚠️ MISSABLEAndrea's optional job after Party Hard: Felipe is at a bar in Millport (Lummox). He's gone for good once Power Play starts. Killing him pays $20,000; sparing him unlocks Collect Felipe's Gift.
  5. This Is a Free Call From… ⚠️ TIMEDAfter Child's Play, switch to Lucia and take the 'Pick up Shayna' call as soon as the hourglass appears, before any other main job.
  6. Do 'both together' stuff before Making GainzFrom Making Gainz until VCIA Takedown, Jason and Lucia can't be seen together in public (except their Y Hotel room). Do any two-character activities before it.
  7. Francisco's LMG-6 (VCIA Takedown) ⚠️ MISSABLEOn the ground during the last fight as Jason. Pick it up before you get in the van.
  8. Save before the last Chapter 4 missionThe story has more than one ending and no replay — keep a manual save before the chapter's final mission.
  9. Pilot Pete's timed deliveries ⏱️Three timed delivery runs in Chapter 4 with deadlines of 20:00, 14:00 and 08:00 in-game. Make each one on time for $20,000 per run.
  10. Ultimate Edition boat at Washington BeachReports conflict on whether it's only available in Chapter 4 — use it now to be safe.

Chapter 5

  1. Free garages: NE 15th Street & Pegassi ResidencesOperation Savage Glory gives you both.📍 Map pin
  2. Finish every character questlineAndy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret each have a trophy — finish them before the final mission.
  3. Save before the final missionKeep a save right before the last mission in case the ending locks a character.
  4. Cheats unlock after the storyCheats only work after the main story, each tied to a challenge — and you can't save with one active.
  5. $2 million savings ending ⚠️ MISSABLEReported: in Protective Custody, don't choose Proceed / 'Meet Abe' until you have $2 million in BuckMe Family Savings — choosing it earlier starts the final mission and loses that ending.
  6. Best ending checklist (reported)From a Section VI breakdown of the 14 endings: 1) relationship heart full (4/4); 2) both Jason and Lucia on the 'Professional' criminal profile — killing and chaos push it toward Hardened, Violent, Psycho, and 'Irredeemable' is permanent; 3) $2 million in BuckMe Family Savings — money moved there can't be withdrawn, and you can't save while a cheat is active; 4) in the finale: at the truck pick Shoot, on the bridge pick Swim For It. Keep a manual save before the last mission so you can try other endings.
