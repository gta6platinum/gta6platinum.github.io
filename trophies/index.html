This Was No Boat AccidentBronzeRecover the treasure from all 7 shipwrecks for Kenton. He leaves after Chapter 1 and comes back later.

Hot TogetherBronzeLose a 4-star wanted level with your partner. Bank vaults always give 4 stars, so it often comes during a vault job.
Retail TherapyBronzeWith your partner, rob 3 shops in a row while still wanted. The north Vice City jewelry stores are close together.
SneakthiefBronzeRob a jewelry store without being seen: pick the back door with the Snap Gun and open the safe with its code (Richardson Jewelry works well). Save first, go in daytime.

The Goodtime StateBronzeUncover the whole map. A plane is the fastest way.
Whip It GoodBronzeScan 100 different vehicles with WAiNK. Standing by a busy road is the fastest way.
Little BoxesBronzeBuy all 8 garages in the Vice City Real Estate app (about $160,000 in total).
On the SpotBronzeRegister 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app. Use cheap cars or bikes and keep garage space free.
Treat Yo SelfBronzeSpend $100,000 in shops in total. Guns, car mods and tattoos add up fastest.
Catch Me If You CanBronzeSteal a passenger jet parked at a boarding bridge at VCIA (gates E3 and E4 are easiest): board through the tunnel, go upstairs to the cockpit, reverse out, take off and fly about 1 km away.
Challenging TankBronzeSteal a Rhino tank from the south of Mile Zero Air Station (Mariana County) and shoot some enemies — you get 4 stars on entering
In The Beginning...BronzeGo to the Rooftop Bar on Ocean Beach, buy a cocktail (e.g. the Snowbird) and look out over the Ocean Beach strip while you drink it. A nod to GTA Vice City.
Capital OffenseBronzeIn the car showroom, hack the blue Bravado Banshee on display and drive it out through the showroom glass. A nod to GTA III.
Let's Go BowlingBronzeTake all three valuables from Kingpin Bowling (Kolt & Associates, Port Gellhorn) and lose the police.
Personal BestBronzeMax the Strength stat for both Jason and Lucia. Training together at a gym trains both at once.
Actions Speak LouderBronzeMake 500 choices that affect your Criminal Profile. Most players get it naturally.
Bad Luck, I GuessBronzeUnlocks after Credit Check (Chapter 3), which gives you the Dye Pack Jammer. Go alone as Lucia to the Payday Loans store in Little Cuba (the prologue store), rob it, and lose the police ON FOOT: no car, bike or boat, or it won't count. Security is high: wear a mask, and take out the guard ahead and the one on the right, then use the snap gun on the side door.
The Truth Is Out ThereBronzeFrom Chapter 2, alone, after 2 AM: road northwest of the Starlet Motel (Kelly County). Find the flashing car, follow the path into the woods to the blue light, help the dropped woman, walk back to her car — you get abducted (trophy). Random: if the car isn't there, drive off and return. Bonus: in the alien dream, count the light bars by the keypad — that 4-digit code is different in every playthrough, so write yours down. Then go to South Point Airfield (restricted site east of the Manatee Motel, Hamlet), climb to the roof, go down to the Restricted Area keypad, enter your code and carry the alien outside: permanent max health, stamina and focus boost. Can also be done after the story.
